3. Check your understanding

Try each question before you open the answer.

  1. Which two substances does a plant take in for photosynthesis, and where does each come from?

    Show answer

    Carbon dioxide, from the air through the stomata; and water, from the soil through the roots.

  2. Write the word equation for photosynthesis.

    Show answer

    carbon dioxide + water → glucose + oxygen (using light energy, in the presence of chlorophyll).

  3. A pupil says: "Plants breathe out oxygen for us." Explain what is right and what is misleading about this.

    Show answer

    Plants do release oxygen, but as a by-product of making their own food, not "for us". They also respire, using some of that oxygen themselves, all day and all night.

  4. Explain why palisade cells are found near the top of a leaf rather than the bottom.

    Show answer

    They contain most of the leaf's chloroplasts, and the top of the leaf is where the light is strongest, so placing them there means more light energy reaches the chlorophyll.
